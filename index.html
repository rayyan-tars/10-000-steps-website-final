Introduction

Hello! My name is Rayyan, and I am a Grade 10 student. This e-portfolio project focuses on the importance of walking 10,000 steps daily, a simple yet effective way to enhance our physical and mental well-being. In today's fast-paced world, many of us, especially teenagers, find ourselves spending excessive time sitting—whether in classrooms, at home, or while using our devices. This sedentary lifestyle can lead to serious health issues, including obesity, diabetes, and heart disease.

Walking is a natural and accessible form of exercise that can be easily integrated into our daily routines. By committing to walk 10,000 steps each day, we can significantly improve our health, boost our mood, and increase our energy levels. This project aims to raise awareness about the benefits of walking and encourage everyone, regardless of age, to adopt this healthy habit.

Throughout this website, you will find valuable information about the health benefits of walking, how it can help teenagers avoid obesity, the barriers that prevent people from walking, and the role of community programs in promoting walking habits. Let's embark on this journey towards a healthier lifestyle together!
